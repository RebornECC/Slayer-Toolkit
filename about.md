# Slayer Toolkit

Tools for people who grind hard levels.

## What you get

- **Death graph.** A bar for every percent of the level. The three worst spots are red, and you see best percent, average death percent and the total death count.
- **Notes.** Write a note for a percent, like "43% hold the click longer". A few percent before that spot the note shows at the top of the screen.
- **Progress.** For each 10% of the level, how often you got through it in your last 10 tries, a hint if you are ready for a full run, and your best percent for each of the last 7 days.
- **Session stats.** Deaths, play time and best percent since you started the game, plus a local rank and badges for demons you beat.
- **Break reminder.** A message after the number of minutes you choose, so you do not burn out on a grind.

## Extras in settings

- **Retry delay.** Wait a set time before the level restarts after a death.
- **Stream mode.** Hides the attempt counter and shows a session timer.
- **Silent mode.** Turns the level music off, sound effects stay.

## How to use

1. Start a level and press pause.
2. Press the **Slayer** button in the bottom right corner.
3. Switch between Deaths, Notes, Progress and Session at the top.

## Good to know

- Deaths in practice mode and test mode are not counted.
- Up to 5 notes per level.
- Everything is saved on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
