# Changelog

## v1.2.1
- The death strip is now off by default, you can turn it on in settings

## v1.2.0
- Levels tab: your recent levels with best percent and deaths
- Copy summary button for sharing your progress
- Session goal with a message when you reach it, and a New best message
- Practice tip on the Progress tab: where to start practicing the weakest part
- Early death warning after 5 deaths below 10% in a row
- Death strip at the top of the screen, red where you die most
- Profiles: Wave, Ship, Timing and Marathon presets

## v1.1.0
- New Progress tab: consistency of each 10% segment over the last 10 tries, a "ready for a full run" hint, best percent per day
- Session tab now shows your rank and badges
- Retry delay: wait before the level restarts after a death
- Stream mode: hides the attempt counter and shows a session timer
- Silent mode: level music off, sound effects stay

## v1.0.0
- First release
- Death graph by percent with the worst spots marked
- Notes tied to percents, shown before the spot
- Session stats and a break reminder
